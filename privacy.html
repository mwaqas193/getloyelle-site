Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data Loyelle handles, why, how long it is kept and the choices people have.

Who we are

Loyelle is a loyalty and rewards app for Shopify stores, operated by Codera. In this policy "we" means Codera, "merchant" means a store that installs Loyelle, and "customer" means a shopper of that store.

For customer data, the merchant decides why and how it is used and we process it on the merchant's behalf. For merchant account data, we are responsible directly. Questions go to hello@getloyelle.com.

What we process and why

DataWhy we need it
Customer name and emailTo identify the loyalty member, show them in the merchant's customer list and send loyalty emails
Shopify customer ID, tags and account stateTo link the member to the store's customer record and apply the merchant's earning rules
Birthday (day and month only)To award a birthday reward, when the customer chooses to give it
Order ID, totals, line items and refundsTo award points for purchases and take them back on refunds
Points, rewards, discount codes and VIP tierTo run the loyalty program
Referral details, including a friend's emailTo deliver referral rewards to both people
One-way hashes of IP address and deviceTo detect referral fraud. We never store the raw IP address or device identifier
A record of loyalty emails sentSo the merchant can see what a customer was sent
Merchant store name, domain and contact emailTo provide the app and contact the merchant about their account

We do not collect phone numbers, postal addresses or payment details. Payments for Loyelle itself are handled by Shopify.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not use customer data for anything other than running the merchant's loyalty program.
  • We do not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Referral fraud checks can hold a referral reward for the merchant to review.
  • The storefront rewards panel loads no third-party trackers.

Marketing emails and consent

Loyelle sends loyalty emails for the merchant, such as points earned, reward reminders and referral invitations. Every email has an unsubscribe link, customers can choose which kinds of email they receive, and those choices are applied straight away.

How long we keep data

DataKept for
Loyalty profiles, points and rewardsWhile the merchant has Loyelle installed
Raw copies of Shopify webhook messages30 days, then emptied automatically
A single customer's personal dataRemoved when Shopify sends an erasure request or the customer is deleted in Shopify
Store access tokensDeleted immediately when the app is uninstalled
All personal data for a storeRemoved when Shopify sends the store erasure request, 48 hours after uninstall

After erasure, points history stays as anonymous accounting records with no name, email or other identifier attached.

Who else processes data

We use a small number of service providers to run Loyelle. Each one only processes data on our instructions.

  • Shopify, the commerce platform the app runs in.
  • Our hosting and database provider, which stores the app's data.
  • Our email delivery provider, which sends loyalty emails.
  • Integrations a merchant switches on, such as Klaviyo or Gorgias. Data goes to these only when the merchant connects them.

The current list of providers and their locations is available on request and is given to merchants before any change.

Your rights

Customers can ask to see, correct or delete their data. Because we act for the merchant, the fastest route is to contact the store you shopped with. The merchant passes the request through Shopify and Loyelle acts on it automatically. You can also write to us and we will forward your request to the merchant.

Merchants can export their customers, points history and rewards from the app at any time.

Security

How we protect data is described on our security page.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell merchants by email before the change takes effect and update the date at the top of this page.
