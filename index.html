The ledger

Points are money. Loyelle treats them that way.

Balances are never edited in place. Every change is a new line in an append-only ledger, so you can always explain how a customer got to their balance.

  • Refunds claw back what was earned. The value comes from the original award, not from today's rules.
  • Unused codes return the points. A cancelled redemption puts the balance back.
  • Nothing is counted twice. Repeated webhooks and retries never create duplicate points or duplicate tier rewards.

FAQ

Questions merchants ask first

Is the free plan really the full product?

Yes. The free plan has the same features as the paid plans, including VIP tiers, referrals, analytics, the API and webhooks. Plans differ by monthly order volume, branding removal and the kind of support you get.

Can I move from another loyalty app?

Yes. Loyelle imports customers, point balances and tiers by CSV, with ready-made mappings for Smile, Yotpo and LoyaltyLion exports. You can also export everything from Loyelle at any time.

What happens to points when an order is refunded?

Loyelle removes the points that order earned, valued at the rate that applied when they were awarded. Partial refunds remove a proportional share.

Will it slow down my storefront?

The launcher is a few kilobytes and the rewards panel loads only when a customer opens it. The storefront widget makes no third-party tracking requests.

Does it work with Shopify POS and the new customer accounts?

Yes. Customers can earn and redeem in store with Shopify POS, at checkout, and inside customer accounts through a dedicated loyalty page.

How are monthly orders counted?

Loyelle counts orders placed in the calendar month that have a customer email, because those are the orders that can earn points. Orders from guests with no email do not count toward your plan.

What happens if I go over my plan's orders?

Nothing breaks. Orders keep syncing and customers keep their balances and can still redeem. New earning pauses until you upgrade or the month resets. On Plus, extra orders are billed at $20 per 100.

What currency am I billed in?

Plans are priced in US dollars and charged every 30 days on your Shopify bill.

How is customer data handled?

Loyelle stores only what the program needs, keeps each store's data separate, and honours Shopify's data request and erasure webhooks.
